Multiview Favorites

Favorited Live TV channels for the selected Emby user are matched to Dispatcharr channels by channel number. The lowest-numbered matches (up to the stream limit) become the tiles of a Dispatcharr Multiview layout, which you watch in Emby like any other channel.

Changes are pushed a few seconds after the user favorites or unfavorites a channel, when this page is saved, and hourly.

Dispatcharr Multiview

Multiview Favorites

Each multiview channel follows one Emby user's favorite Live TV channels. They're matched to Dispatcharr channels by channel number, and the first ones (up to the stream limit) become the tiles of a Dispatcharr Multiview layout, which you watch in Emby like any other channel.

Changes are pushed a few seconds after a user favorites or unfavorites a channel, when this page is saved, and hourly.
